          How payments work
          • Your wallet signs one USDC transfer of exactly $1.00 to the board's receiving address. The address is shown before you sign.
          • It is not a token approval. It can't move any other tokens or any more USDC, and it expires about 2 minutes after you sign.
          • A payment service (x402) submits the transfer on Base and pays the gas. You only need USDC.
          • Your takeover is queued only after the transfer settles.
          • The code is public: github.com/cwendt6/bounce-board.
