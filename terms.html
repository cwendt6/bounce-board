3. What you buy

A purchase (a "takeover") gets you a place in the queue to show your card on the box. Here is how it works:

  • Queue order. First paid, first shown. Your place is set by the time your payment settles and our server records it. Ties are broken by an internal ID.
  • Minimum time. When your turn starts, your card shows for at least 60 seconds, unless we remove it (see section 8).
  • Corner bonus. Each time the box hits a corner while your card is showing, your card is protected for 60 seconds from that hit. The box does not pass to the next buyer during that window. A corner hit inside the window starts a new 60-second window.
  • How long you keep it. After the minimum time, you keep the box until the next paid buyer's turn starts, subject to any corner protection. If nobody is in the queue, your card stays up until someone else buys.
  • Estimates. Start times shown for the queue are estimates. They already count the minimum time and corner protection of the cards ahead of you. They can change, for example if we remove a card ahead of you.
  • Timing source. Our server's clock controls all timing. What you see on your screen may lag slightly.

You do not buy any ownership in the site, any number of views, clicks or visitors, or any result.

Restored cards. If we remove the card that is showing and nobody is in the queue, we may bring back the most recent previous card that we did not remove, free of charge. The timing rules in this section apply to a restored card too. So the next buyer's turn starts only after the restored card's minimum time and any corner protection. A restored showing is not a new purchase and gives that card's buyer no extra rights.
